CoDiR Training Objectives (Section 2.3)
L = ½ (Lsup + Lunsup)

Lsup = λ1LCE + λ2LDice + λ3LclDice + λ4LBoundary

Lunsup = λuLrefineunsup + λcLconsunsup  (λu = λc = 0.25)

Lrefineunsup = E[ w · (LBCE(ywu, yr) + LDice(ywu, yr)) ]

Lconsunsup = LBCE(ysu, ywu) + LDice(ysu, ywu)
